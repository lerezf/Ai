# Monetization
**Topic:** monetization  **Confidence:** medium-high on the basics (Creator Hub), medium-low on pricing/benchmarks (blogs)

## The primitives (Creator Hub)
- **Passes:** one-time purchases (permanent perk, restricted area access).
- **Developer products:** repeatable purchases (currency, ammo, potions, boosts).
- **Premium Payouts:** passive Robux based on Premium subscribers' playtime in your game; no setup, and it works even with nothing for sale.
- Also: subscriptions, paid access, ads, UGC items. Managed pricing tool helps find price points.
- DevEx: need at least 30,000 earned Robux to exchange for real currency (Creator Hub economics page). Rate cited as roughly $0.0035-$0.0038 per Robux (third-party).

## How to apply
1. Build and test the free loop first; price after players are attached.
2. Anchor on one method per game (passes OR dev products), layer the rest.
3. For sticky games, repeatable dev products tend to out-earn passes (endsights); idle/collector games do well with accelerators and rare items.
4. Price tiers (rowatcher): ~50-100 Robux impulse items, ~500-1,500 Robux premium/VIP passes.
5. Premium Payouts reward session length, so design for engagement time.
6. Rank/status passes work because players like outranking peers.
7. Offer value without breaking balance, especially in PvP.

## Policy / risk
- Players downvote monetization they dislike.
- Chance-based monetization (loot boxes/random items) must be transparent and used responsibly; some mobile-F2P tactics are not recommended on Roblox. Re-check current policy before shipping.
