# Growth, Launch, and Metrics
**Topic:** development  **Confidence:** medium (Deconstructor of Fun is the strongest source here)

## Key ideas
- Roblox growth is closer to YouTube SEO than mobile user acquisition: the algorithm and organic discovery matter most (Deconstructor of Fun).
- Top teams are small: often capped around ~20 people, many 10-15 (Deconstructor of Fun).
- Track: qualified playthrough (clicks, explores the page, plays meaningfully), D1 retention, D7 retention, 7-day playtime, conversion rate (Deconstructor of Fun). Roblox docs also list ARPPU.
- Think "session value" not just session length.

## Launch playbook (rowatcher, medium-low confidence)
1. Soft launch to find tutorial length and mobile-control problems.
2. Start creator/influencer outreach 4-6 weeks before launch with a press kit (overview, screenshots, trailer, early access link).
3. At roughly hour 72, decide: full push (retention above ~25%, like ratio above ~70%), tune and hold, or stop.
4. Spend small to "pulse" traffic (a few hundred to ~1,000 Robux) to test retention before spending big (low-confidence blog advice).
